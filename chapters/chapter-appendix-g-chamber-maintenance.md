# Appendix G: Chamber Cleaning and Maintenance Protocols

## G.1 Standard Maintenance Schedule

### Daily

- Visual inspection of chamber window (cleanliness)
- Check coolant flow and temperature
- Review etch rate trends (alert if >5% drift)

### Weekly

- Gas line filter inspection and replacement if clogged
- Pump backing oil change check
- Pressure gauge calibration verification

### Monthly

- Chamber pressure leak test (target: <0.1 Pa·L/s)
- Electrode visual inspection (erosion assessment)
- Liner thickness measurement (if installed)

### Quarterly (Every ~1000 wafers)

- Electrode cleaning or replacement
- Liner replacement (if quartz; typical life 500-1500 wafers)
- RF matching network tuning verification

### Annually

- Chiller servicing and refrigerant check
- Pump oil change
- Complete chamber calibration and optimization

## G.2 Electrode Erosion Management

**Erosion rate:** 200-400 nm per 1000 wafers (Cl₂ chemistry typical)

**Action levels:**
- 500-1000 nm erosion: Monitor; no action yet
- 1000-2000 nm erosion: Schedule replacement within 1-2 months
- >2000 nm erosion: Replace immediately (performance degrading)

**Replacement procedure:**
1. Vent chamber
2. Remove power and coolant connections
3. Extract electrode via chamber top
4. Clean or replace as needed
5. Reinstall and purge with Ar
6. Pump down and resume operation (test recipe on dummy wafer first)

## G.3 Chamber Conditioning After Maintenance

**After liner or electrode replacement:**

1. Pump down and leak test (should be <0.1 Pa·L/s)
2. Etch 3-5 dummy wafers at nominal recipe
3. Measure depth on test wafer #3 and #5
4. If within ±5% of expected depth, chamber is conditioned; resume production
