# Glossary: 3D NAND and Deep Trench Etch Terminology

## A

**ARDE:** Aspect Ratio Dependent Etching. Etch rate depends on local trench aspect ratio; high-AR trenches etch 2-4× slower than low-AR trenches.

**Aspect Ratio (AR):** Ratio of trench depth to diameter. 3D NAND: 50:1 to 300:1+.

**Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM):** Surface profiling technique; measures trench bottom residue and sidewall roughness.

## B

**Bias Voltage:** DC voltage applied to wafer; determines ion energy (50-150 V typical).

**Blocking Oxide:** High-k layer between control gate and charge trap (5-10 nm).

**Bowing:** Non-linear sidewall deflection in deep trenches; concave or convex.

**Boron Trichloride (BCl₃):** Boron chlorine source; provides selectivity control and sidewall passivation.

## C

**C₄F₆:** Perfluorobutane; selective SiO₂ etch chemistry with carbon polymer passivation.

**CCP:** Capacitive Coupling Plasma (standard for 3D NAND).

**Charge Trap (CT):** Nitride layer storing charge distributed across trap sites.

**Chlorine Radical (Cl·):** Primary neutral etchant in Cl₂ chemistry.

**Cluster Tool:** Multiple chambers connected by transfer arm for sequential wafer processing.

**Critical Dimension (CD):** Feature size (300-500 nm for channel holes; ±15-30 nm tolerance).

## D

**Debye Length (λ_D):** Plasma sheath characteristic length (10-100 μm typical).

**Dielectric Barrier Discharge (DBD):** Plasma source using dielectric between electrodes.

**Dynamic Selectivity Switching:** Multi-stage etch with chemistry changes for optimal selectivity at each layer.

## E

**Electron Temperature (T_e):** Characteristic energy of electrons in eV (typically 2-5 eV for etch discharges).

**Etch Rate:** Material removal rate (μm/min typical).

**Etch Stop:** Material halting etch progress (tungsten common for 3D NAND).

**Endpoint Detection:** In-situ measurement (OES) to determine etch completion.

## F

**Floating Gate (FG):** Isolated polysilicon charge storage (classic NAND; phasing out).

**Fluorine Radical (F·):** Highly reactive etchant for SiO₂; from F₂ or F-hydrocarbons.

## G

**Gate Oxide Integrity (GOI):** Quality metric; degradation causes charge loss.

**Gas Distribution:** Inlet design ensuring uniform pressure and flow across chamber.

## I

**Impedance Matching:** Tuning RF network to 50 Ω; minimizes reflected power.

**ICP:** Inductive Coupling Plasma (higher density than CCP; 13.56 MHz typical).

## K

**Knudsen Number (Kn):** Ratio of mean free path to chamber dimension; determines flow regime.

## L

**Liner:** Protective coating (quartz/ceramic) protecting chamber walls.

**Loading Effect:** Pattern-dependent etch variation (dense vs. sparse).

## M

**Mean Free Path (λ):** Distance molecule travels before collision (~1-2 μm at 50 Pa).

**Microloading:** Aspect ratio-dependent etch within pattern.

**Multi-Level Cell (MLC, TLC, QLC):** Storing multiple bits per cell (2, 3, 4 bits respectively).

## N

**Neutral Depletion:** Reduction of radical concentration with depth; dominant at AR > 100:1.

**NAND String:** Series-connected memory cells.

## O

**OES:** Optical Emission Spectroscopy (monitors plasma light; primary endpoint detection).

## P

**Plasma Potential (V_p):** Floating plasma voltage (~10-20 V above ground).

**Pulsed RF:** RF power modulated with duty cycle (50-80%); reduces thermal load and residue.

## R

**Remote Plasma Ash (RPA):** Separate ash chamber; more effective than in-situ.

**Residue:** SiOxCly polymers at trench bottom; requires post-etch ash removal.

**RF Power:** 13.56 MHz electromagnetic power driving plasma.

## S

**Selectivity:** Ratio of etch rates for different materials.

**Sheath:** Plasma boundary region with accelerating electric field for ions.

**Sidewall Passivation:** Protective layer (SiCl_x or CFx) preventing lateral etch.

**Sputtering:** Physical removal of atoms by energetic ions.

## T

**Thermal Runaway:** Uncontrolled temperature rise from positive feedback.

**Trap Generation Rate:** Defect creation rate in oxide during high-temperature stress.

**Tunnel Oxide:** Thin SiO₂ layer (5-9 nm) enabling tunneling charge transfer.

## V

**Volatility:** Tendency of byproduct to remain gaseous (high = less residue).

## W

**Word Line (WL):** Gate electrode in NAND row.
