# Appendix A: Plasma Chemistry Equilibrium Data

## A.1 Cl₂ Dissociation and Ionization

**Electron impact reactions (at electron temperature T_e = 2-5 eV):**

| Reaction | Threshold (eV) | Cross-section (Å²) | Rate Coefficient |
|----------|----------|----|-----------|
| e + Cl₂ → 2 Cl + e | 2.5 (dissoc.) | 1-3 | ~10⁻⁹ cm³/s |
| e + Cl₂ → Cl₂⁺ + 2e | 11.5 (ioniz.) | 0.1-1 | ~10⁻¹¹ cm³/s |
| e + Cl → Cl⁺ + 2e | 13.0 | 0.5 | ~10⁻¹¹ cm³/s |

**Key result:** Dissociation dominates over ionization; Cl· production ~100-1000× higher than Cl⁺

## A.2 Recombination Processes

**Ion-electron recombination (dominant process):**

$$Cl_2^+ + e \\rightarrow Cl + Cl \text{ (or other products)}$$

Rate coefficient: ~10⁻⁷ cm³/s (temperature dependent; decreases with T_e)

**Radical recombination (produces residue):**

$$Cl + Cl \\rightarrow Cl_2$$

$$SiCl_2 + SiCl_2 \\rightarrow Si_2Cl_4 \text{ (polymer)}$$

## A.3 Silicon Etch Reactions

**Primary reaction:**

$$Si + 2 Cl \\rightarrow SiCl_2$$

$$SiCl_2 + 2 Cl \\rightarrow SiCl_4$$

**Enthalpy:** -430 kJ/mol (exothermic; reaction favorable)

**Activation energy:** ~20-40 kJ/mol (temperature-dependent)

## A.4 SiO₂ Etch Reactions

**Fluorine-based (highly efficient):**

$$SiO_2 + 4 F \\rightarrow SiF_4 + 2 O$$

Enthalpy: -800 kJ/mol (very exothermic; rapid)

**Chlorine-based (requires ion assist):**

$$SiO_2 + n Cl + \\text{ion impact} \\rightarrow \text{SiOCl}_x + \text{other products}$$

Much slower; enthalpy ~-200 kJ/mol

## A.5 Ion-Assisted Etch

**Sputtering yield (atoms removed per ion) as function of ion energy:**

| Energy (eV) | Si | SiO₂ | Si₃N₄ |
|----------|----|----|-------|
| 50 | 0.1 | 0.01 | 0.05 |
| 100 | 0.3 | 0.05 | 0.15 |
| 200 | 0.8 | 0.2 | 0.4 |
| 500 | 2.0 | 1.0 | 1.2 |

**Total etch rate:** Chemical etch rate + (Sputtering yield × Ion current)

*Note: All data empirical or literature-derived; actual values vary with plasma conditions, temperature, and chamber design.*
