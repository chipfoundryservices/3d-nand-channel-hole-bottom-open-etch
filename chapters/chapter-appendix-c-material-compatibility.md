# Appendix C: Material Compatibility Matrix

## C.1 Etch Rate Selectivity Matrix (Cl₂-based Chemistry)

**Etch rates (μm/min) at standard 3D NAND conditions (5 kW, 60 Pa, 80°C):**

| Material | Cl₂-Based | SiO₂-Selective Phase | Si-Selective Phase |
|----------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Polysilicon | 0.8 | 0.3 | 1.5 |
| SiO₂ | 0.15 | 1.2 | 0.15 |
| Si₃N₄ | 0.4 | 0.2 | 0.6 |
| Tungsten | <0.01 | <0.01 | <0.01 |
| Aluminum | 0.05 | 0.03 | 0.08 |

**Key selectivity ratios:**
- Si/SiO₂: 5-8:1 (Si-selective phase)
- SiO₂/Si₃N₄: ~6:1 (oxide-selective phase)
- Si/W: >100:1 (good etch stop)

## C.2 Fluorine-Based Selectivity Matrix (C₄F₆ + Ar)

| Material | C₄F₆ + Ar |
|----------|-----------|
| Polysilicon | 0.2 (protected by C-polymer) |
| SiO₂ | 1.8 |
| Si₃N₄ | 1.2 |
| Tungsten | <0.01 |

**Selectivity SiO₂/Si:** >8:1 (excellent for oxide-selective phase)

## C.3 Etch Stop Effectiveness

| Etch Stop Material | Selectivity vs. Si | Selectivity vs. SiO₂ | Notes |
|------------------|----------|----------|-------|
| Tungsten | >100:1 | >50:1 | Gold standard; most reliable |
| TiN | >50:1 | >20:1 | Good; less common |
| Mo | >100:1 | >50:1 | Effective but rarer |
| SiCN | ~10:1 | ~1-2:1 | Moderate effectiveness |
